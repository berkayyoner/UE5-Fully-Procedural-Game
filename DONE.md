- Armour covers what it covers: every piece has a material (padding, mail, plate) and the bones under it, and a blow is met by the piece at the bone and point where it lands, by the way it lands (an edge, a point driven in along the blade, or a blunt blow). Plate takes 95% of an edge, 90% of a point and only half of a blunt blow, so cuts glance off it and maces and kicks still tell; mail stops an edge (85%) but not a point (45%); padding softens all three. Bare places take everything: the face under an open helmet, the neck, the groin, the hands, under the arms where the pauldrons open and high on the sides under a breastplate, the thighs above greaves. ArmourInfo prints what the worn armour takes on each part of the body from the front, back, side and below.
- Senses, knockouts and choking: every body has a consciousness of 100. Blows to the head take it by the jolt that gets through (half through plate, nothing below a tap, about a third of a point per joule), a hand closed on a living throat takes 14 a second from an even match (a grab near the throat takes the throat first), and it comes back 3 s after the last of them. Below two thirds the body is groggy like a dazed one (weaker arms, slower, worse balance); at nothing it drops senseless, lets go of what it holds and lies limp until a third of its senses are back, about 9 s; choked on past that it dies. Seen in four fights: knockouts from one heavy or two middling head blows, and every one of them came round.

- Broken bones: a crushing blow (a mace, a fist, the back of an axe, a blade that struck flat or glanced; an edge that bites cuts instead) that gets through the armour to a limb's long bones breaks them, an arm from 380 J and a leg from 520 J for a body of middling strength (stronger bodies' bones take more, a blow on a hand, a foot or a collarbone counts half). Measured in four fights: 4 arms broken in 116 blows (of 56 blunt or glancing ones). The physics ensure on getting up still shows in about one fight in four.
