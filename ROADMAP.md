# Roadmap

Order of work for the night of 2026-11-08, most felt first.

1. Quick fixes: "Farewell", enemy bars without mana and stamina, Shift in the fight stance, a boulder let go with both hands, sword-like weapons held upright, looser running arms, quieter and correct footsteps, subtler footstep effects that grow with step speed, a heavier two-handed weapon in one hand.
2. A physical body: held weapons are physics bodies of their own that collide with bodies and the world and pull on the hand with their mass; nothing passes through a body; the body sways and carries its momentum.
3. Death and injury: no sudden death without losing the head; going down, crawling, trying to get up, bleeding out. A lost weapon arm drops the weapon; unarmed or armless NPCs flee.
4. Strikes: downward blows that reach the ground, the spine bending with vertical swings.
5. Grabbing: severed limbs and bodies with real mass, no glitches when held.
6. The main menu works.
7. Dialogue: a Skyrim-like screen with ornate rules and a selector, the speaker's name beside the topics, subtitles; NPC remarks to the player and NPC-to-NPC conversations as subtitles.
8. A test landscape replacing the template level: a landscape master material blending the Megascans surfaces with height maps, macro variation against tiling, realistic scale; trees, rocks, ruins, a camp with food and drink on tables, weapons and armour laid out for tests.
9. Footprints shaped like a real boot sole; more packs from the library.
